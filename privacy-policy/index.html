Privacy Policy

24×7 DOBI

Last updated: 15 Nov 2026

At 24×7 DOBI, we respect your privacy and are committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share information when you visit https://24x7dobi.lk, contact us or request our services.

1. Who We Are

24×7 DOBI is a Sri Lanka-based assistance service providing technology, digital, travel and local coordination services.

  • Website: https://24x7dobi.lk
  • Business operator: ceylonog pvt ltd
  • Contact email: poke@24x7dobi.lk
  • Country: Sri Lanka

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Your WhatsApp number, Telegram username or email address.
  • Your service requirements, correspondence and messages.
  • Location or travel details you voluntarily provide.
  • Business or technical information necessary for an agreed service.
  • Transaction, invoice and payment-confirmation information.
  • Website usage information collected through actually configured analytics tools.

Do not send passwords, complete payment-card information, medical records, ID documents or other highly sensitive material through ordinary messaging unless necessary and a secure submission method has been agreed.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and assess requests.
  • Prepare quotations and coordinate or deliver agreed services.
  • Communicate service progress.
  • Process payments and maintain relevant business records.
  • Resolve complaints and disputes.
  • Maintain website and account security.
  • Understand website usage and improve the website.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use contact information for unrelated marketing without an appropriate legal basis or permission.

4. WhatsApp and Telegram

Our website links to WhatsApp and Telegram. When you contact us through those platforms, the relevant provider may independently process your communications under its own terms and privacy policy.

We may retain relevant conversations for support, quotation records, billing and dispute resolution, subject to our retention practices and applicable law.

5. Website Analytics and Cookies

This static website does not currently load third-party advertising pixels, remarketing trackers or non-essential cookies. If analytics technologies such as Google Analytics 4 are configured in the future to understand website visits and measure contact-button clicks, we will update this section and obtain consent where required by applicable law.

6. Sharing Information

Where reasonably necessary, we may share relevant information with:

  • Hosting, technical and communications providers.
  • Authorized team members and contractors.
  • Travel, transport or other third-party providers involved in a request.
  • Payment processors.
  • Professional advisers or public authorities where legally required.

We share only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. We do not sell customer contact information or enquiry details to unrelated data brokers.

7. Third-Party Services

Some requests require independent third-party providers. We will explain relevant coordination arrangements where appropriate. Third parties may maintain their own privacy notices and legal responsibilities.

8. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards such as access controls, account security and limited internal access. No internet-based communication or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, accounting, security, applicable legal duties and dispute resolution. We delete, anonymize or securely dispose of information when no longer needed and when legally permitted.

10. International Data Transfers

Communications, hosting, analytics or service providers may process information outside Sri Lanka. Where applicable, we take measures required by relevant data protection laws for international transfers.

11. Your Privacy Requests

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about personal data we hold, corrections, deletion where applicable, withdrawal of consent for consent-based processing, or restrictions on certain processing activities where applicable.

Contact poke@24x7dobi.lk. We will review and respond according to the rights and obligations in force at the time of the request.

12. External Links

Our site may link to external websites and messaging services. We are not responsible for independent privacy practices of other organizations. Review their policies before sharing information.

13. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended primarily for adults and authorized representatives. We do not knowingly solicit children's information for independent service transactions. Where appropriate, a parent or guardian should contact us on a child's behalf.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy as our operations or legal obligations change. Revisions will appear here with a new update date.
